# Customer Retention and Churn Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges faced by telecommunication companies. Retaining existing customers is often more cost-effective than acquiring new ones. This project analyzes customer churn patterns using Power BI and provides actionable insights to improve customer retention.

The dashboard was developed using the Telco Customer Churn dataset and focuses on identifying key factors influencing customer attrition, such as contract type, payment method, internet service, and customer tenure.


## Objectives

* Analyze customer churn behavior.
* Identify factors contributing to customer attrition.
* Measure overall churn rate.
* Visualize customer retention trends.
* Provide business recommendations to reduce churn.

## Dashboard Components

### KPI Cards

1. Total Customers

   * Displays the total number of customers.

2. Churned Customers

   * Displays the total number of customers who left the service.

3. Churn Rate (%)

   * Shows the percentage of customers who churned.



### Visualizations

#### 1. Customer Churn Distribution

Displays the proportion of customers who churned versus retained customers.

#### 2. Churn by Payment Method

Analyzes churn behavior across different payment methods.

#### 3. Churn by Contract Type

Shows churn trends based on customer contract duration.

#### 4. Churn by Tenure Group

Analyzes churn according to customer tenure periods.

#### 5. Churn by Internet Service

Compares churn across internet service categories.


## Key Findings

### Contract Type Impact

Customers with month-to-month contracts exhibit significantly higher churn rates compared to customers with one-year and two-year contracts.

### Tenure Impact

Customers within their first 12 months are more likely to leave the service.

### Internet Service Impact

Fiber optic customers show the highest churn levels among all internet service categories.

### Payment Method Impact

Customers using electronic check payment methods have a higher tendency to churn.

### Overall Churn Rate

The organization experiences a churn rate of approximately 26.54%, indicating a significant retention challenge.



## Business Recommendations

1. Encourage customers to switch from month-to-month contracts to long-term plans.

2. Improve onboarding and engagement strategies during the first year of service.

3. Investigate service quality concerns among fiber optic users.

4. Promote automatic payment methods to improve customer retention.

5. Implement targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customer segments.


## Outcome

The dashboard successfully identifies customer churn patterns and provides valuable insights for business decision-making. By addressing the identified churn drivers, organizations can improve customer retention, increase customer satisfaction, and enhance long-term profitability.
